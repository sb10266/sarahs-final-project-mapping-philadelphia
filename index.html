🦅The City of Philadelphia🦅

Philadelphia is known as "The City of Neighborhoods." While other cities, including Chicago and New York, are also considered "cities of neighborhoods," only Philadelphia gets the distinctive "The." Neighborhood names are visible on the map, and clicking one shows a quick profile. You can also click the regions in the legend to see a list of all the neighborhoods within it.
